# ADR-0006: The full panel is always available; mastery gates rungs, never access

- Status: Accepted (2026-06-11)

## Context
A training ladder invites the dark pattern of locking the flagship experience
behind progression ("finish 5 drills to unlock the panel").

## Decision
The 3-seat panel **is the product and is always available**. The scenario
ladder is the *recommended* path between panels, driven by "when is your
interview?". Mastery gates govern **rung escalation within a scenario**, never
access to scenarios or the panel.

## Consequences
No engagement-gating debt to unwind later. Drills and ladders must earn usage
by being useful, not by holding the panel hostage.
