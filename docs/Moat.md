# Moat

A raw voice interview is **commoditized** — ChatGPT Advanced Voice Mode and Gemini Live will role-play an interviewer for free, no key required. So the moat is explicitly **NOT** the conversation, **NOT** the prompt text (the [[Leadership Principles]] are public), and **NOT** the transport ([[Realtime Panel]]). It is four things a stateless, agreeable LLM voice structurally *cannot* be:

1. **Calibration** — every answer scored against the company's real rubric, producing a trusted *level* ([[Signal Levels]]) that is consistent across sessions. See [[Judgment Pipeline]].
2. **An adversarial multi-interviewer panel** — three evaluators ([[Bar-Raiser Panel]]) who score **independently** (their disagreement is signal a single chat can't fake), a [[Amazon Bar Raiser]] who can **veto**, and a committee verdict.
3. **Longitudinal memory** — it drills *your* recurring weakest LPs over time, not generic questions.
4. **The honest report** — the exact moment you cracked → your one rep to fix it.

## The compounding asset
The durable advantage is **accumulated per-user gap data + a cross-user calibration corpus** — slow to build, hard to copy. The product gets more accurate at reading "Senior vs SDE II" the more sessions it scores.

## The competitive whitespace
Web research (2025–2026) found **no candidate-facing tool ships a calibrated multi-interviewer panel with a committee verdict.** Final Round AI, Yoodli, interviewing.io, Exponent, Google Interview Warmup — all use a *single* AI/peer interviewer. The only multi-persona "Bar Raiser panel" product (HireReady.ai) is **employer-side**. Details in [[Competitive Landscape]].
