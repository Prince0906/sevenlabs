# Vision

**Build the candidate's confidence speaking under interview pressure.** Confidence — a measurable index ([[Confidence Metric]]) — is the north-star metric, *not* a hire/no-hire score. A hire verdict is a means; the felt, repeatable composure of someone who has already said their stories out loud, under fire, is the end.

## The wedge: tell the truth
Every other tool says *"great answer 👍."* Aloud says *"you'd get rejected — and here's exactly the moment you cracked, and the one rep to fix it."* No mainstream tool grades the **strategic content** of a behavioral answer (the senior-vs-junior [[Signal Levels|signal]]) — they grade delivery and call it a day. That gap is the opening (see [[Competitive Landscape]]).

Positioning: **"Practice harder than the real interview, walk in confident."**

## The non-negotiable principle
"Harder than the real thing" must **never make an already-anxious person more anxious.** This is enforced *mechanically*, not by good intentions:
- a difficulty governor with a **warm default** and adversarial "boss mode" as opt-in;
- a mid-round circuit breaker;
- the Bar-Raiser veto only fires at follow-up depth `>= 2` (no vetoing the first vague sentence);
- the [[Confidence Metric]] is **never shown as a single falling judgmental number** until its validity is proven.

The thesis is evidence-backed end to end — see [[Evidence Base]].

## Target user
A SWE with a FAANG interview in 4–8 weeks, anxious about behavioral rounds, who has *typed* prep into ChatGPT but never *spoken* it aloud, and wants something under ~$25 versus a $500/hr coach.
