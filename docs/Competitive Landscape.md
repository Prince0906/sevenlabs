# Competitive Landscape

**Bottom line:** as of 2025–2026, **no candidate-facing tool ships a calibrated, multi-interviewer adversarial panel with a committee verdict.** Every named candidate tool uses a *single* AI or peer interviewer. That whitespace *is* the [[Moat]].

## The field
| Tool | Voice / real-time | Scoring | Panel? | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Final Round AI** | yes (live Copilot + async mock) | clarity/confidence/relevance score | single interviewer | freemium; Copilot ~$96–148/mo |
| **Yoodli** | yes, live AI roleplay | filler/pace/clarity + enterprise scorecards | single persona at a time | free (5 lifetime) → $8–20/mo |
| **interviewing.io** | yes (live **humans** from FAANG) | detailed written feedback | one interviewer/session | ~$179–300+/session |
| **Exponent** (absorbed Pramp) | yes (peer) | AI-graded vs hiring rubrics | no | free (~5/mo) → ~$12–79/mo |
| **Google Interview Warmup** | speech→transcript | **none** (insights only) | no | free |
| **Pathrise** | human mentor | mentor feedback | no | ISA (~9% of first-year salary) |
| **ChatGPT Advanced Voice / Gemini Live** | yes | none formal | single | free / paid tier |

## The lone multi-persona panel — and why it's not a competitor
**HireReady.ai** runs a virtual hiring panel (a "Bar Raiser", a "Candidate Advocate", a "Structured Interviewer") with debrief reports — but it is **employer-side** (for running real hiring), not a candidate practice tool. (Separate from BarRaiser.com, an employer interview-outsourcing platform.) Research-side, dual-agent adversarial mock interviews exist in papers (arXiv 2506.16542) but aren't productized for candidates.

## So the wedge is
A **candidate-facing**, calibrated [[Bar-Raiser Panel]] that grades the senior-vs-junior [[Signal Levels|signal]] and issues a committee verdict — plus transparent pricing weaponized against Final Round AI ([[Pricing and BYOK]]).
