# Aloud — Home

**Aloud** is voice-first FAANG interview prep that tells SWE candidates the truth. You practice behavioral answers *out loud*; every answer is scored against the company's real rubric, so you learn whether you read as **New Grad, SDE II, or Senior**. The north star is **measurable speaking confidence under pressure** — not a hire/no-hire vanity score.

### The one-paragraph mental model
A candidate talks; their **voice goes browser→OpenAI directly** ([[Realtime Panel]]) while only **JSON checkpoints** reach Aloud. Three AI interviewers ([[Bar-Raiser Panel]]) — each owning different [[Leadership Principles]] — interview, drill, and hand off. Afterward, an **off-band [[Judgment Pipeline]]** scores the transcript, applies a deterministic Bar-Raiser veto, and writes a committee verdict + a [[Confidence Metric]] + one drill to run next. The whole thing is built to make you *more* confident, not more anxious.
